# Contributing

Thank you for helping build safer, clearer knee-injury knowledge.

## Choose the right contribution path
- **Evidence/guideline/research update:** open an Evidence Update issue and include a DOI, PMID, guideline URL, or other traceable source when possible.
- **Incorrect or unclear content:** open a Content Correction issue and identify the page/claim.
- **Product/website/workflow idea:** open a Product / UX issue.
- **Patient experience:** during the foundation phase, do **not** post private histories, records, MRI/DICOM, or identifiable patient stories in public GitHub issues. A privacy-preserving intake flow will be built before structured patient stories are collected.

## Medical-content contributions
Separate:
1. what the source actually reports;
2. population/context;
3. interpretation;
4. limitations/uncertainty;
5. conflicts with existing sources.

Do not strengthen a conclusion beyond its source.

## AI-assisted contributions
AI is welcome for search, summarization, translation, structuring, and drafting. The contributor/reviewer remains responsible for source fidelity. Never cite a paper that cannot be verified to exist and support the claim.

## Pull requests
Keep PRs focused and explain:
- the problem being solved;
- what changed;
- medical/safety/privacy impact;
- evidence/references used;
- review still required.

## Language
English is the canonical project language during the foundation phase for international collaboration. Vietnamese is an early-priority translation. The architecture should add languages without duplicating the underlying evidence model.

Patient experience and clinical expertise provide different kinds of knowledge. Neither should be dismissed; neither should be misrepresented as the other.
