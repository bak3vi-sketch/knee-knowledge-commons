# User Journeys

The website should organize information around what a person is trying to do, not around medical taxonomy or repository structure.

## Core patient journeys

### 1. I just injured my knee
Needs: what information to collect, what kind of assessment exists, what uncertainty is normal early on, what questions to prepare, and reviewed urgent-care guidance when applicable.

### 2. I have a diagnosis or radiology report
Needs: plain-language explanation of terms, associated-injury concepts, what the report can/cannot establish, and questions for the treating clinician.

### 3. I am comparing treatment paths
Needs: options, goals/trade-offs, population-specific evidence, uncertainties, questions to ask, and clearly separated community experience.

### 4. I am preparing for surgery
Needs: preparation checklist, expectations that are broadly evidence-supported, questions about procedure/rehab, and local-clinician instructions taking priority over generic content.

### 5. I am rehabilitating
Needs: phase concepts, objective milestones, common barriers, reasons plans differ, and help preparing questions for the rehabilitation professional. The product must avoid prescribing individualized exercise progression.

### 6. I want to return to running or sport
Needs: explanation of criteria-based versus time-only decisions, relevant assessments, uncertainties, reinjury-prevention concepts, and questions for the care team.

### 7. Something is not going as expected
Needs: safe explanation of common categories of problems, reviewed escalation guidance, and structured preparation for clinical follow-up — not remote diagnosis.

## Contributor journeys

### Patient/community member
Share lessons and lived experience through a guided, privacy-preserving form. They should not need GitHub or medical vocabulary.

### Clinician
Suggest a correction, attach a source, comment on applicability, or review medical meaning through a web workflow; GitHub remains optional.

### Researcher
Submit DOI/PMID/guideline/source, highlight relevance or limitations, and review extracted evidence. Agent automation should do the repetitive formatting.

### Maintainer/reviewer
Work with issues, PRs, evidence records, validation tools, and agent-created proposals in GitHub.

## UX rule

At every major page ask: **“What decision, question, or next conversation is this helping the user prepare for?”** If a page exists only because a folder exists, redesign it.
