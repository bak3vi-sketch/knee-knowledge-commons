These are methodological and clinical starting points for the project foundation. Inclusion here does not mean every future claim should rely on these sources; topic-specific evidence must be reviewed for the population and question at hand.

## Important scope warning

A guideline can exclude clinically important groups. For example, the AAOS 2024 acute isolated meniscus guideline explicitly does not cover several categories including chronic/degenerative tears, root tears, re-tears, and meniscal injuries occurring with concomitant injuries such as ACL tears. Agents and authors must read scope/exclusion criteria before reusing recommendations.

## Source-handling rule

Prefer linking and original synthesis over copying copyrighted text, tables, or figures. Record enough provenance that a reviewer can inspect the original publication.
