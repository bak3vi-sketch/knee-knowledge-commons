# Medical Safety Policy

> **Project-owner Vietnamese version:** [MEDICAL_SAFETY.vi.md](MEDICAL_SAFETY.vi.md)

Knee Knowledge Commons provides education and evidence navigation. It does not provide individualized diagnosis or prescribe treatment.

## Allowed patient-facing behavior
Content and AI may explain medical terms, summarize reviewed evidence with sources and limitations, describe common care pathways, preserve uncertainty, and help people prepare questions for qualified clinicians or rehabilitation professionals.

## Not allowed
The project must not diagnose an individual from chat/symptoms/images, prescribe a personalized procedure or rehabilitation program, promise outcomes, present patient anecdotes as efficacy evidence, or encourage delaying appropriate professional care.

## High-impact medical changes
Any change that alters practical meaning about diagnosis, treatment, surgery, rehabilitation, prognosis, contraindications, return to activity, or urgent-care escalation requires:
1. traceable source support;
2. explicit limitations/uncertainty;
3. qualified human review before publication as approved knowledge.

AI may draft or check these changes but cannot be the sole approving authority.

## Imaging
The MVP must not present AI interpretation of raw MRI/DICOM as a clinical diagnosis. Explaining terminology in an existing radiology report may be supported as education, with uncertainty and appropriate referral back to qualified professionals.

## Conflicting evidence
When reputable sources disagree, preserve the disagreement. Describe meaningful differences in population, intervention, comparator, outcome, timing, methodology, and certainty rather than forcing one answer.

## Urgent-care information
Any feature surfacing emergency warning signs or local care pathways must use reviewed, jurisdiction-appropriate sources and receive dedicated safety testing before release.

Clinical guidelines are decision-support tools rather than universal protocols. The project helps people understand decisions; it does not make those decisions for them.
