# Code of Conduct

Knee Knowledge Commons brings together patients, clinicians, researchers, developers, caregivers, and other community members. Disagreement is expected; disrespect is not.

Contributors must:
- discuss claims and evidence rather than attack people;
- respect lived experience without presenting it as universal proof;
- avoid shaming people for treatment choices, disability, recovery speed, care access, or financial constraints;
- avoid promotional pressure for specific clinics, clinicians, products, devices, or procedures;
- protect patient privacy;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- accept correction when a citation or summary is shown to be inaccurate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate medical misinformation, spam, and undisclosed commercial promotion may be removed and may result in contributor restrictions.

Moderation should prioritize community safety, evidence integrity, and a welcoming environment for people with different levels of medical and technical knowledge.
